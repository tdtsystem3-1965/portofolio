About Me (&)
My Journey

30 years as a surgeon and audiologist in China, now rebuilding that same precision in IT and clinical research in the U.S.

'83

Foundation in Medicine

Began a Bachelor's in Clinical Medicine at Chongqing University of Medical Sciences — the start of a 30-year clinical career.

'88

Residency & Master's

Trained as an ENT resident, then earned a Master's in Otolaryngology researching the electrophysiology of the facial nerve.

'95

Senior ENT Surgeon

Practiced Otology and Rhinology at West China Medical Center, specializing in Functional Endoscopic Sinus Surgery (FESS).

'02

First Steps Abroad

Visiting Scholar at Dalhousie University, Canada, researching temporal resolution — the first of several international research postings.

'03

Co-Founder, Hearing Center

Co-founded one of the first dedicated audiology clinics in China, growing it over a decade into a premier clinical and research facility.

'07

Research in the U.S.

Visiting Scientist at the House Ear Institute in Los Angeles, researching artificial hearing implants and learning to run a hearing-science lab — my first working chapter in the U.S., and the seed of the move that followed.

Now

Rebuilding in Massachusetts

Based in Shrewsbury, MA, earning CompTIA A+ and Google AI Essentials certifications and applying a surgeon's precision to a new career in IT and clinical research coordination.
